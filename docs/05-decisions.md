# Decision log

Format: ID, decision, why, alternatives, reversible?. Add new entries instead of editing history. Defaults chosen where the brief left a choice open are marked **(default)**; the user may overrule them.

**D1 (default). Language and engine: TypeScript + three.js, web-first, no game engine.** The prototype is already three.js; the terrain, cel shader and placement code port with near-zero rewrite; hot reload makes procedural art fast to iterate; a pure-TS sim is trivially unit-testable; one codebase serves browser, desktop (Tauri/Electron) and later mobile web. Alternatives: Godot 4 (GDScript, good editor but a rewrite and weaker headless sim testing), Unity (heavy, C# rewrite). Reversible: moderately; the sim and data are engine-agnostic by design, so a port would reuse design, data and tests-as-spec.

**D2 (default). Platform: desktop browsers (Chrome/Edge/Firefox/Safari recent), mouse + keyboard first.** Touch is a later milestone. Offline: runs from a static build; PWA/offline caching in the polish milestone.

**D3 (default). UI: Preact + plain CSS over the canvas.** Small, fast, component model for the many panels. No CSS framework.

**D4 (default). Validation and tests: zod for data, Vitest for tests, ESLint boundaries for sim purity.**

**D5. 2.5D camera: narrow-FOV perspective at isometric pitch, yaw snaps of 90 degrees.** From HANDOFF.

**D6. No external art; all assets generated in code.** From HANDOFF. Hand-made models may be added later for buildings/units only.

**D7. Data-driven content, deterministic tick-based sim, saves are local JSON.** From HANDOFF.

**D8 (default). Fixed sim step of 10 ticks per second; 1 game day = 600 ticks (60 s at 1x); season = 20 days (20 min); year = 80 days (80 min at 1x).** Speeds 0/1/2/4x. Tunable constants in `src/sim/constants.ts`.

**D9 (default). One continuous world, not a separate city scene.** The city is a territory of hexes around the capital on the same map; zoom out to see the world. Buildings are placed on buildable hex cells inside territory (territory grows with Grand Coop level). This differs from Rise of Kingdoms' fixed-slot city view, and matches "smooth zoom from city to world" in HANDOFF.

**D10 (default). Single player map size: "Standard" about 9 to 12 regions on radius 60 hex map; "Small" radius 40 for quick runs.** Configurable at new game.

**D11 (default). Combat is auto-resolved, round-based, from counts, counters, hero stats and skill; battles are shown with a short procedural animation, not simulated in 3D.**

**D12 (default). Offline catch-up is capped (8 real hours) and safe: gathering and construction progress, raids do not occur and human outposts only grow up to a cap.** Preserves the "returnable" pillar without punishing absence.

**D13 (default). Working title, now "ChickenWars" (D45).** The visible name lives in `package.json`, `index.html`, `public/manifest.webmanifest`, `src/ui/strings.ts` (`gameName`) and the docs.

**D14 (default). Win condition "Reign" and soft failure.** See overview. Provisional.

**D15 (default). Extra rare resource called "chili peppers"** (hero equipment upgrades, tier-3 troops, late research). Four basic resources are feed, twigs, pebbles, golden eggs.

**D16 (default). Pathfinding on a hex nav graph (micro hexes) derived from the terrain logic layer, with cost by terrain and level difference; marches are interpolated along the path and snapped to the height field in render.**

**D17. Two scenes: Base (city) and World map. Supersedes D9.** The user's build goals require a separate base scene (buildings placed, built and levelled in 3D) and a world-map scene (navigation, resource nodes, humans, marches). Both are rendered in the same three.js renderer with the same cel and outline materials; the player switches between them with a HUD toggle. The sim stays one `GameState`. The city keeps its hex-cell placement from `systems/city.md` but lives on its own generated base terrain; the world map uses the island terrain from `04-terrain.md`. Reversible: yes, scene switching is a render and UI concern only.

**D18 (default). Orchestration model split per the user:** opus agents orchestrate and design, sonnet agents write code and visually review builds, haiku agents review, research and QA. Build runs autonomously.

**D19 (default). Logic layer lives in GameState.** `world.regionOf/level/biome/flags/navCost` and ramps are copied into state at new game (plain number arrays, about 11k cells at radius 60, well under the 2 MB save budget). Sim stays self-contained; render regenerates the height field and checks `terrainHash`. Clarifies architecture section 3 ("only logical cell data lives in world").

**D20 (default). Logic-layer determinism by restricted maths.** The logic layer uses integer hash noise and only `+ - * /`, floor, sqrt, abs, min, max (IEEE-exact in every JS engine); no sin/cos/exp/pow/hypot. The height field and meshes may use any maths.

**D21 (default). `advance` mutates state in place** and returns events; deterministic for the same `(state, commands)`. Cheaper than copying the state 10 times a second.

**D22 (default). Human level and loot scaling (user goal 6).** Every hostile POI has a level 1..25 (camps from tier plus growth, lairs and bosses `tier*5`). Loot quantity and rare-drop weights scale with `poi.level - townHallLevel`, clamped, data in `world.json.lootTables` and `balance.json.lootScaling`. See `07-build-contract.md` section 6.

**D23 (default). Base scene terrain.** The base (city) scene is a generated plateau of radius `territoryMaxRadius` (16) hex cells with forest, rock and water cells for adjacency bonuses, from `src/gen/base`. Territory radius from the Grand Coop limits buildable cells. The capital also occupies one world cell (`world.capitalCell`) where marches start and end.

**D24 (default). Screenshots via playwright-core driving the system Google Chrome** (`tools/screenshot.ts`, no browser download), saved to `docs/screenshots/`.

**D25 (default). Sim core conventions (M0/M2).** (a) `advance` runs systems with `state.tick` = the tick being processed, then increments it; day rollover happens when `tick % 600 === 0` (tick > 0); `season.day`, `dayOfSeason`, `year` are 0-based. (b) Grand Coop is placed at the base-grid centre cell index (`centreCellIndex(R)`), not literal cell 0, because contract cell order starts at a corner. (c) `WorldLogicInput` carries `capitalCell` (needed to seed `world.capitalCell`); `navCost` 0 means blocked. (d) Clan `heroStartLevel` is applied at new game (start hero level 1 + bonus). (e) `costAtLevel`/`ticksAtLevel` use repeated multiplication, not `Math.pow`. (f) Data schemas are `.strict()`: unknown keys fail validation, so a new field needs a schema + doc update. Effects: building effects are level-indexed arrays; clan/research/talent share one `EffectSchema` union; skills use `CombatEffectSchema`; world events use their own union. (g) Loot rare entries carry a `bias` (weight multiplier `1 + bias*delta*rarePerLevel`, floored at 0); the roller is an M5 task. (h) `GameClient.frame` while paused runs one tick if commands are queued, so the player can act while paused. (i) Heroes' talent tree id equals hero class. (j) `@types/node` added as a devDependency for tools/tests. (k) `catchUp(state, ticks)` in `tick.ts` implements D12 chunking with `flags.safeMode`.

**D26 (default). Render foundation conventions (art agent).** (a) Outline hull thickness is in world units at a 30-unit camera distance and scales linearly with distance (clamped 0.2x..6x), so lines stay about 3 px at any zoom; building/marker 0.06, units 0.04, wall 0.05, outline colour = vertex colour x 0.3. (b) Visual stages: non-hall buildings change at L3, L5 (extra storey), L7, L10 (banner); Grand Coop stages at L5/10/15/20 plus a gilded L25. (c) Marches show 3 to 7 figures by troop total (<=10, <=50, <=200, <=1000, more). (d) Camera pitch 38 degrees, yaw via Q/E, wheel zoom exponential and continuous with a snap hint to the three levels; right/middle drag pans. (e) Default roof colours vary per family (barracks slate, stable/archery orange, etc.) unless `roofAccent` is given. (f) Fog-of-war texture: R8 with 0/128/255, bilinear. (g) Hover = swapping to a shared brightened cel material (`setHover`), not per-object uniforms. (h) Icons are plates with ink rim drawn at 64-unit design space; unknown ids return ''.


**D26 (default). Terrain port defaults (M1).** (a) Sea cells have `regionOf = 65535` in typed arrays and `-1` in `toPlainLogic`. (b) Ramps connect flat *patches* (connected same-level cells), not only macro regions, so level blobs inside a region are reachable; `ramp.regionA/regionB` may be equal. Ramp cells (high and low side of the link) carry flag bit3; `canStep` allows a one-level step if either cell is a ramp cell. (c) Lakes are round basins inside interior regions (not whole regions), lake and river water cells have flag bit4 and are not walkable/buildable; biome of water, fords, bridges and level-0 coast cells is 0 (beach). (d) Scaling: radius <= 34 keeps the prototype seed spacing (about 60 micro-regions); above that the lattice spacing is derived from a target region count (10 at R40, +0.22 per radius step, so about 9-13 at R40 and 17-19 at R60), levels follow mean distance to the sea (6.5 hexes per level) instead of region-graph depth. (e) Islands start: K land masses on a ring, joined by 3-cell-wide level-0 bridge strips (`logic.bridges`, flagged ramp); the sim decides whether bridges are usable before unlock. (f) River start: source at the peak region, descends to the coast; water cells level 0 with 3-hex-wide terraced banks, 4-cell fords with a flat plaza of radius 2. (g) Frozen north: effective altitude shift `floor(snowBaseline * (1 + 2*north) + 0.5)` levels in biome selection only. (h) Slope guarantee: ramps over 0.6 are widened (5 passes, cap 5.5), re-routed (up to 4 candidate border edges), lengthened (cap 9) and finally forced linear, so the verified maximum is always below 0.6; `ok` means slope only (`stats.lowRise` counts ramps climbing under 1.0 on thin plateaus). Default cliff width 0.15 (was 0.22). (i) Field sample spacing 0.3, capped at 720 samples along the longer axis. (j) Placement outputs are plain arrays (`rocks` with quaternion, `trees`, `tufts`, `stones`, `reeds`); timing uses an injected `now` clock, never `performance.now` inside `src/gen/terrain/field.ts`.

**D27 (default). City, economy and training conventions (M3 sim).** (a) Market: there is no market building in `buildings.json`; `marketTrade` unlocks at Grand Coop level 5 (`MARKET_TH` in `systems/economy.ts`), trades at 3:2 (get = floor(amount*2/3)), cooldown `balance.marketCooldownTicks` per *given* resource (`city.market[give]`), chili peppers cannot be traded, clan `tradeStep` 1 gives 5:4 (2 steps 6:5 → 5:6 give). (b) Grand Coop gained a `popCap` effect (10 at TH1, +2 per level) so troops can be trained before the first house; population used = troops (home + wounded + training + marching, by `popCost`) + 2 per completed non-hall building. (c) Production = base * max(floor, (1 + adjacency + research + clan + buffs) * season * (damaged ? 0.5 : 1)); the floor is `balance.productionFloor`. Production is credited with a floor-difference over the 600-tick minute (`fpForTick`) so rates have no rounding bias. Clan/tech `adjacency` effects scale the adjacency bonus by (1 + pct/100). Buff stats `prod_all`, `prod_<res>` or `<res>` modify production; `healMult` modifies healing. (d) Caps: chili peppers is raised only by its own base cap, never by `all` feed silo effects. Stock above the cap (refunds) is never reduced. `ResourceOverflow` is emitted once when stock first reaches the cap (`lost` may be 0). (e) Starvation is stateless: at 0 food with an unmet upkeep, desertion happens on ticks divisible by `starvation.intervalTicks`; warnings fire on crossing 20% of the cap and on reaching 0. Marching troops eat from city stock (the `supply` field is left to the marches agent). (f) Build queue: a job exists per construction/upgrade, new buildings exist at level 0 until done, queue limit is builders + `buildQueueExtra`, jobs start FIFO when a builder is free; `BuildingCompleted` fires for every completion and `BuildingUpgraded` additionally for level > 1. Demolish and training-building demolish are refused while a job exists. (g) Training picks the eligible building with the shortest queue; duration is fixed when the batch starts; `woundedShare` = 0.6 + 1.5% per level of the best nursing nest + research `woundedRatio`, max 0.85; wounded above nursing nest capacity (0 without an nursing nest) die at day end (`WoundedDied`, lowest tier first); healing uses optional `GameState.healAccum` (x1000 remainder) and heals highest tier first. (h) Balance bot: scripted opening reaches TH3 at 10.6 minutes without data tuning.

**D27 (default). M4 world/nav conventions.** Nav edge rule: blocked if navCost 0; level change only if |diff|==1 and one cell has the ramp flag, cost x1.5; bridge cells are plain walkable cells (no extra lock). Locked regions block unless `allowLocked`. Tiers = walkable region distance +1 (max 6), tier <=2 unlocked; combat unlocks neighbours on boss kill. March speed: 100 ticks/cell at troop speed 10 (`nav/constants.ts`), terrain divisor 1+0.05*(navCost-1) (max 1.5), x1.5 across ramps. Gathering is `gatherRate x 1 resource/troop/minute` x hero/season/difficulty/buff multipliers. Fog recomputed every 10 ticks (capital 6 or best lookout perch, marches 4, cavalry scouts 7, plus research/talent vision).

**D28 (default). M9 sim: quests, win, progression.** (a) Steps: building steps (`buildingBuilt` counts instances at level >= 1, `buildingLevel` best level) are absolute against the city; every other step is a delta from the counter value recorded when the quest started (`state.quests.base[quest]`, additive optional field), so earlier progress never pre-completes a quest. `counter` steps are absolute unless `delta: true`. (b) The quests system first derives counters from the tick's events: `trained_<troop>`, `researchDone`, `heroRecruited`, `bossKilled_t<tier>` (tier from the POI), `raidsWon`/`raidsLost`, `giftGivenEv` (any positive `LordRepChanged`; the `giftGiven` step reads max(`giftGiven`, `giftGivenEv`) so it works before allies bumps its own counter). Counters other systems already write are untouched. (c) One main chain (`quests.active`) may advance several quests in one tick; triggered side quests (`trigger: {counter, value}`, e.g. `city_in_peril` at `raidsLost >= 3`) live in `quests.side` beside the chain and pay `rewards.troops` (ally help). Rewards are paid in the tick the quest moves to `completed`, so once only; resources go through `depositResources` (capped, `ResourceOverflow`), heroes through `heroes.grantHero`, `xp` goes to the first hero. (d) New events `QuestStarted`; the win event is the existing `Won`. (e) Win "Reign": `bossKilled_t<finalTier>` (final tier = highest boss POI tier on the map, 6 on full maps) AND Grand Coop at the building's max level (25); sets `flags.won` once, `counters.reign = 1` (completes the `reign` quest), `Won`, chronicle. `continueSandbox` sets `flags.sandbox`. (f) Lose: none in v1 (D14: the capital cannot fall while the game is returnable); the `flags.won` end screen is the only terminal state. (g) Score is cosmetic (`computeScore`, weights in `progression/score.ts`, integer maths, difficulty scaled 80/100/125%, time bonus only when won). (h) Cosmetic profile (`progression/profile.ts`): hard win unlocks banner `banner_ember_gold`, a normal or hard win unlocks portrait `portrait_laurel`; nothing affects a new game's power. (i) Clan effect reads go through `clanBonus` (progression/clan.ts); `classDef` and `buildingHp` have no consumer yet (`CLAN_EFFECT_CONSUMERS`). `dailySeed(y,m,d) = hash32('daily', y, m, d)`. (j) `tools/validate-data.ts`: quests with a `trigger` are exempt from "reachable from the first quest".

**D28 (default). M6 heroes/research conventions.** (a) Talent points are derived (`level - 1 - spent`), not stored; a node needs `requires.points` already spent in its tree plus listed nodes at rank >=1. `hero.skillRank` is stored but recomputed from talents (`1 + skillRank nodes`, max 3) on `heroTalent` and reset by `respec`, so combat reads it unchanged. `respec` and `equip` are refused while the hero is on a march (`on_march`). (b) Items store percentages; each upgrade adds +10% of the base stat; upgrade to +n costs gold 100*n*rarity and chili peppers n*rarity (common=1..epic=4), needs a built Owl Academy (constants in `heroes/constants.ts`). (c) Worm Tavern: needs a built Worm Tavern, candidates = unowned `recruit.kind 'tavern'` heroes ordered by hash(seed, cycle, id), count from `heroCandidates`, recomputed only when `tick >= refreshAt`; `recruitHero` limited by `balance.heroMax`; `grantHero` (quests, ruins, shrines) adds unique heroes once. clan `heroStartLevel` applies only to the starting hero (init). (d) Research vocabulary reuses `classAtk`/`classDef` and adds `classHp` instead of new `troopAtk/Def/Hp`; added `gatherMult marchSlots trainSpeed healMult buildSpeed wallHp towerAtk supplyMult fogReveal`. `researchBonus(state, kind, filter?)` sums research only and clamps to +-100 (overrides in `RESEARCH_CAPS`: buildSpeed 25, marchSlots 1, visionRadius 4, fogReveal 3, tradeStep 2, raidWarning 1800 ticks, woundedRatio 30, trainTime 50, supplyMult 50). Quests derive the `researchDone` counter from `ResearchCompleted`. (e) Not yet consumed: see M6 report (combat class mods, rageGain, buildSpeed, marchSlots, gatherMult, trainSpeed, healMult, wallHp, towerAtk, supplyMult, fogReveal, talent-global trainTime/upkeepMult/capMult).

**D29 (default). M7/M8 raids and allies conventions.** (a) Raids: a camp launches when active, past `raids.graceDays` (12 days of quiet start), off cooldown, `strength >= tier raidThreshold x difficulty raidThresholdMult` (hard 0.9, x0.9 more near a hostile lord), within `raidRange` (18) of the capital, city defence power < strength x difficulty x 0.8, plus a 20% roll every 60 ticks; at most 2 raids in flight. Defence power = home troops + lent allied troops + tower atk x4 + (tower+wall hp)/10 + idle heroes (atk+def+hp/10); damaged defences count 50%. Arrival = launch + max(travel at 100 ticks/cell, warning); the warning fires `raidWarningTicks x warningMult + research` before arrival. Battle: walls+tower hp absorb 40% of raider damage, towers hit for atk x2 per round, best idle hero leads, 2 more fight as ally heroes, clan buildingHp/classDef applied. A lost raid loots max(5%, 20% x surviving raiders share) of stock after feed silo protection (2.5% per feed silo level, max 25%; chili peppers never), damages 1-2 non-hall buildings and never destroys; a won raid cuts the camp 25%. `cityInPeril=1` after 3 lost raids via private tally `raidDefeats` (quests derives `raidsLost/raidsWon` from RaidResolved). (b) Ambush: once per gather stop, chance 8% x (tier-1) x season humanGrowth x difficulty (max 60%), only in tier>=2 regions without a camp ever cleared (sticky counter `regionCleared_<id>`); ambushers are the tier camp at 50% strength; a beaten march turns home with survivors. (c) Safe mode (offline): no raids launched, raids on the road turn back, no ambushes, no requests posted, attack marches fight only at >=1.2x power else abort with `AttackAborted` (hook in combat.ts). (d) Lords: reputation clamps -100..100; gifts earn `value x 0.05` rep (likes x1.5, dislikes x0.5, x0.6 per earlier gift that day, min 1); decay 1 per 5 days while rep > 25; trade is a fixed lot of 100: wary 1:1 once/day, friendly 1.25..1.48 (3/day), allied 1.5 (5/day), +0.1 per clan tradeStep, +-0.05 for liked/disliked given resource, hostile refuses. Requests every 1-3 days per non-hostile lord (max 3 open, weighted seeded pick, none while offline); `allyRequest.choice` is `accept|refuse|fulfil` optionally `:<requestId>`; deliver is manual, clearCamp/defend/escort complete automatically (escort = a scout/garrison march reaches the village); urgent unanswered expiry -5, 3 refusals in a row -10. Clearing a camp within 8 cells of a village gives +3 (+tier-1). (e) Stage perks: friendly reveals the lord's region; allied adds the village area to explored fog, lends 50% of its army to city defence, sets counters `heroOffer_<lord>` and `allyTech_<lord>` (hooks for heroes/research; events `LordPerk`). Seasonal gift at season start from friendly+ lords (their liked resources). (f) Rally: needs an allied lord; the march waits in phase `done` at the capital up to `rallyWaitTicks`; lords arrive after 60 ticks per cell; combat adds 60% of each joined lord's army and 2 ally heroes; losses are split pro rata so lord troops never join the player's pool; loot 60% player / 40% lords by power (permille, exact), lords' loot converts to rep, +2 join, +5 on a kill.

**D28 (default). Render scene conventions.** (a) The renderer exposes `frame(dtMs)`; `main.ts` drives both client and renderer (no second rAF). (b) Camera rigs are created on scene activation and disposed on deactivation (the rig listens to wheel/pointer on the shared canvas), with position/zoom saved per scene; yaw resets. (c) Terrain chunks use a 108-sample (about 32 unit) grid, LOD strides 1/2/4 chosen by camera distance with skirts instead of stitching. (d) World props are not instanced per chunk; one InstancedMesh per variant is refilled from per-chunk arrays when the visible chunk set changes (keeps draw calls near 2 per variant); unexplored cells hide their props and markers. (e) Far from the camera (> about 34 units or world zoom) markers draw only their floating icon. (f) Marker picking is screen-space nearest-projected-position, building picking is ray vs AABB, ground picking is a ray march on the height function. (g) Locked regions are darkened in vertex colours (chunks rebuild when unlock flags change), not in the fog texture.

**D30 (default). UI conventions for the M6/M8/M9 panels.** (a) Esc with nothing else open (no panel, march dialog, placing or selection) opens the pause menu and pauses; closing restores the earlier speed (`ui/menu.ts`). (b) The raid banner is derived from `state.raids` entries with `warned === true`, not from UI store, so it survives reloads; auto-pause on `RaidWarning` stays a setting. (c) Raid and ambush reports show the verdict from the player's side (the player is the defender). (d) Text seeds are hashed with `hash32('seed', text)`; all-digit seeds are used as numbers; Daily seed uses the local calendar date via `platform/profile.ts`. (e) The profile (`hl-profile`) is recorded once per run (key seed+difficulty+start+clan in `hl-profile-recorded`). (f) Rallies start from the Diplomacy panel against the currently selected camp/lair/boss; the march dialog dispatches `startRally` when its kind is `rally`. (g) The event card is hidden while a modal panel is open (it reappears after closing; the sim expiry still applies). (h) Settings gained `quality` (applied through `renderer.setQuality` in `main.ts`) and `textSize` (`--text-scale`); `colour-blind` adds shape glyphs (rarity, stage, research node, choices) via CSS.

**D31 (default). Audio, PWA and accessibility (M10).** (a) Audio is WebAudio only: SFX recipes in `src/platform/audio/sfx.ts`, a seeded xorshift pentatonic sequencer in `audio/music.ts` (one loop per season, major for spring/summer, minor for autumn/winter, quiet bus at 0.22 x music volume), `audio/bind.ts` wires unlock-on-first-gesture, settings, one delegated click listener for UI buttons, `client.on` events through `eventSounds`, and season music. Repeats are throttled per sound. (b) `Settings.muted` now defaults to false: the AudioContext only exists after a user gesture, so the game is silent until interaction without making the player hunt for an unmute. (c) Icons: CLAUDE.md forbids image files, so `vite.config.ts` has a small plugin that emits `icon.svg` from an inline string at build time (not committed) and injects the manifest/icon links. The manifest (`public/manifest.webmanifest`) references `icon.svg` only. (d) `public/sw.js` is hand-written, cache-first for same-origin GETs; the plugin replaces `__BUILD__` (hash of built files) and `__PRECACHE__` after the bundle is written; registration is production-only (`src/platform/pwa.ts`). Vite `base` is `./` so the build works from any path. (e) Accessibility: reduced motion defaults to the OS preference on first run and a `.reduced-motion` rule disables all animations and transitions; `document.body.dataset.colourblind` and `dataset.reducedMotion` are exposed for the renderer.

**D30 (default). Effects wiring and bot tuning.** (a) Every stat is summed once in `src/sim/bonuses.ts`: research (capped by `RESEARCH_CAPS`) + clan + (for city-wide stats) hero talents, then capped again as a total (buildSpeed total 25%). (b) City-wide talent effects (trainTime, upkeepMult, capMult, buildSpeed) are summed over ALL owned heroes, rank-weighted, max 25 per stat (simplest; heroes at home would make the economy flicker with marches). (c) `playerSideMods` is the only source of player combat mods; for rallies the weights use the player's own stacks and the result applies to the whole side. (d) Ally techs are normal research entries with `requiresFlag`; ally heroes use `recruit.kind 'ally'` and the player claims them with `acceptHeroOffer` (counter `heroOffer_<lord>`: 1 offered, 2 taken); they may exceed `heroMax`. (e) A camp raids only when the Grand Coop >= its tier's cityLevelRange start - 2. (f) Build jobs take `buildSpeedPct` into account when they start; `BuildJob.ticks` then holds the effective duration. (g) Balance bots live in `tools/bot/` and use only commands plus read-only sim helpers (resolver preview).

**D32 (default). M10 pacing retune (seed 1337/42/7 small Normal, bot).** (a) Tier-2 troops unlock at building level 3 (was 5), their techs need Owl Academy 2 (was 3) and take 2000/2400 ticks; tier-2 food costs 55/55/90, spur soldier and yolk marksman cost no gold (boar knight 10), train times 80/85/110 ticks per head, upkeep 0.7/0.7/1.1. (b) Output tables: farm L3+ x1.4 (+ extra L4+), lumber L1-2 +6%/+21% and L3+ x1.9, pebble pit and mine raised at all levels. (c) Warchief Gorrak (tier-2 boss) is 100 Pitchfork Chicks + 50 egg slinger (was 130 + 60): the kill still needs about 125 troops with a tier-2 core (bot casualties 24 to 50% of the army, hero level 8 enemy). (d) Bot: tier-1 troops capped at 130, tier-2 techs bought first and the research slot held for them, farm-first goals when food income is thin, army mass-up for the boss (gatherers and outbound camp attacks recalled, loss cap 0.8 for bosses), guard share taken from the whole army. (e) Ally hero offer is only set for lords that have a hero (`allies/perks.ts`). Results: TH3 8.1 min; TH5 38.0 / 39.1 / 39.4 min and first boss 64.3 / 56.5 / 67.7 min (seeds 1337 / 42 / 7). Variance between bot variants of +-10 min on the boss is normal (mass-up timing); seed 7 sits close to the 70 min edge. Early raids at TH4 (tier 2-3 camps with str 600-1475 vs a thin garrison) are still lost on seeds 42 and 7 (4 to 6 losses, minor loot); the M7 year test only runs seed 1337 (0 lost). Left for a later pass: earlier lookout perches or a raid-warning recall in the bot.

**D33 (default). Post-audit gaps: market building, caravan, island bridges, battle brief, boss phase 2, migration.** (a) Market is a real building (`market`, unlock TH5, max 1, 10 levels). `marketTrade` needs a completed market (`locked` otherwise); the new building effect `tradeLimit` caps the amount given per trade (200 at level 1 up to 5000), cooldown stays 600 ticks per give-resource and the 3:2 ratio and clan `tradeStep` are unchanged. This supersedes D27(a)'s "no market building". (b) Caravan event uses the existing `trade` effect for the guaranteed option and one new effect kind `gamble {chance, win, lose}` for the risky option: a seeded roll `hashRand(seed,'gamble',tick)`, losses clamped to what the player has. No temporary caravan target (simplest reading of world.md). (c) Islands bridges: the generator's `bridges` are copied into `world.bridges` (`{id, regionA, regionB, cells, unlocked}`, optional so old saves and bridge-less maps are untouched) and start locked. (d) Boss phase 2: the defender side may carry `enrage {belowPct 50, atkPm 300}`; once at or below 50% of its starting troops it gains +30% attack for the rest of the battle (rage event `boss_enrage`, `BattleResult.phase2Round`). Constants in `combat/modifiers.ts`. (e) `battlePreview(state, draft, poiId)` in `src/sim/combat/preview.ts` runs the real resolver for 9 fixed roll seeds (win chance = share of wins, losses = mean) and returns enemy composition, recommended counter classes (Infantry > Cavalry > Archers > Infantry, siege vs palisades) and phase 2 info; the bot keeps its own single-roll `predictAttack` (now also enraging bosses).
