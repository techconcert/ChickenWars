# Research

Owl Academy (TH3+) researches one tech at a time (two at academy L10). Techs are data. Branches: **Economy** (gather, production, storage, trade), **Military** (troop stats, tier unlocks, training speed, heal), **Defence** (walls, towers, raid warning lead time, garrison), **Exploration** (march speed, vision, supplies, fog reveal radius).

- Cost: resources + ticks (no speedups). Required Owl Academy level by tier: 1/3/5/7/10.
- Effects are percent multipliers or flat bonuses using the shared Effect vocabulary, summed and capped per stat (cap +100% except where stated).
- About 40 techs at launch (10 per branch), tier 1 to 4. Tier 4 needs chili peppers.
- Resetting is not possible; all research is permanent per run. No power creep across runs.
- UI: tree view with branch columns, prerequisite lines, hover for effect and cost, queue indicator on HUD.

Tests: no cycles, all requires exist, caps enforced.
