# Progression, quests and win condition

## Power
City level = Grand Coop level (1..25). Gates: building maxes, territory, march slots, troop tiers, region access (via bosses), research tiers. TH levels above 10 require chili peppers and boss kills (tier N region cleared).

## Tutorial and quests
Chain of 12 tutorial quests through TH5 that teach each system (build farm, train troops, gather, scout, defeat first camp, recruit hero, research, gift to lord). Then **region quests** (clear camp, find ruin, ...) and **lord requests**. Quest rewards: resources, XP, items. Data-driven (`quests.json`: id, title, steps with conditions on state/events, rewards, next).

## Bounty board (rotating quests)
After the tutorial gather quest, a board offers `slots` (3) generated bounties at a time, drawn from a pool of about 790 (`data/bounties.json`, generated by `src/sim/quests/bountyGen.ts`). The pool is template x band x variant: 44 objectives (train a troop, upgrade a building, gather, explore, ruins, research, gifts, lord requests, marches home, battles won, clear a tier-N camp, repel raids), 8 bands by Grand Coop (1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-9, 10-12, 13-16, 17-20, 21-25: Hamlet to Realm) and 3 variants (quick, steady, grand). Counts and rewards scale with the band (`count` and `reward` multipliers; per-template `scale` softens counts for things that are naturally limited, like upgrades), grand variants add XP, chili peppers and an item whose rarity follows the band. Objectives only use `delta` counters, so existing progress never counts. Rules (`quests/board.ts`, `quests/index.ts`): a pick is from the player's band (75%) or the one below, never offered twice (`board.seen`), never the same objective as another active bounty; the board offers one new bounty every `refillDays` (20) days while a slot is free (the first one 10 days after the tutorial gather quest), and a bounty lapses after `lifeDays` (50) days. Seeded with `hashRand(seed, 'bounty', tick, slot)`. New derived counters: `upgraded_<type>`, `upgraded_total`, `trained_total`, `marchesReturned`, `battlesWon`. Bounties are not added to `quests.completed` (the score and end-screen count authored quests); `board.done` counts them. UI: the Quests panel marks them "Bounty" with days left.

## Random events
See `world.md`. Drawn using `hash(seed,'event',day)`.

## Win: "Reign"
Defeat the final region's boss and reach TH25. Shows an end screen with stats (time, battles, resources) and "Continue in sandbox". Soft fail: none (see humans D14). A **Chronicle** log records milestones (screenshot-able summary).

## No power creep
Meta progression between runs is limited to cosmetics unlocks (banner colours, hero portrait variants) tracked in a separate small `profile` record. Nothing that increases starting power.

## Difficulty and scoring
Score is cosmetic (shown on end screen), computed from time and efficiency. Hard difficulty unlocks a banner.

## Tests
Quest conditions evaluate against events; quests chain; win condition triggers once.
